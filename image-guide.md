## Image Specifications

### Recommended Dimensions
- **Service Images**: 800x600px (4:3 ratio)
- **Gallery Images**: 600x400px (3:2 ratio)
- **File Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 500KB for optimal loading

- **Commercial**: Office buildings, warehouses, restaurants

## How to Replace Images

1. **Backup Current Images**: Keep the current images as backup
2. **Prepare New Images**: Resize and optimize your photos
3. **Replace Files**: Simply overwrite the existing files with your new images
4. **Test the Website**: Refresh your browser to see the changes

## Image Sources

### Free Stock Photo Websites
- Unsplash.com - High-quality free photos
- Pexels.com - Free stock photos
- Pixabay.com - Free images and videos
- Freepik.com - Free and premium images

### Professional Photography
- Hire a professional photographer
- Use your own work photos (with client permission)
- Stock photo subscriptions (Adobe Stock, Shutterstock)

## Tips for Good Images

1. **High Quality**: Use clear, high-resolution images
2. **Professional Appearance**: Clean, well-lit photos
3. **Show Action**: Images of technicians working
4. **Before/After**: Show treatment results when possible
5. **Safety Equipment**: Show proper PPE and safety measures
6. **Modern Equipment**: Feature current pest control technology

## File Naming Convention

Keep the same filenames when replacing:
- ✅ `residential-fumigation.jpg` (keep this name)
- ❌ `home-pest-control.jpg` (don't rename)

This ensures all HTML references continue to work correctly.
